summary: Custom PHP MVC chase sheet using Salesforce SOAP APIs for advertising operations follow-up.
---

Maintained, refactored, and extended an existing custom PHP MVC application without a third-party framework. The chase sheet used Salesforce SOAP APIs and SOQL to display opportunity names, statuses, and placement data for ad operations, with email actions for client follow-up.

Added separate chase-sheet pages for different media platforms, including print magazines, and date filters that made it easier to track opportunity status and email clients for missing advertising assets.
