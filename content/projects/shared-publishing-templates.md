## Challenge

Brand sites needed a shared way to build reports and special pages. Typical report and special-page development was taking approximately 1–2 days.

## Approach

Built the first site and a shared Tailwind CSS template, then rolled it out across approximately 12 Joomla brand sites. Modernised those sites through staged CMS upgrades. Documented the shared templates and deployment workflows for remote developers.

## Outcome

Typical report and special-page development fell from approximately 1–2 days to a matter of hours.
