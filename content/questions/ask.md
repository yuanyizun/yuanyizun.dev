## Question

Questions?

## Answer

1. What would you identify the success of the role in the first three months?

2. How is the engineering team structured, and who would this role work with most closely?

3. The job description mentions AI coding tools. How does the team use them, and what does the review and testing process look like?

4. What are the next stages of the interview process, and what should I prepare for the technical assessment?
