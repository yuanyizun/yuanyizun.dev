Built a lightweight CRM with Laravel, PHP, Blade, Tailwind CSS, Alpine.js, and MySQL for a sales team seeking faster daily workflows than Salesforce.

Implemented company SAML SSO, role-based access control, contacts, call lists, events, activity tracking, and follow-up emails, with Redis caching and Laravel Horizon for queued jobs.

Deployed to AWS EC2 through a GitHub-based CI/CD workflow, with Pest unit tests and staging end-to-end and user acceptance testing. Released the first version in approximately two weeks. Adopted for daily use; sales staff reported faster, easier call management.
