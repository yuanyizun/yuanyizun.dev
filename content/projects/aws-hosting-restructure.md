Independently restructured AWS hosting for approximately 150–200 brand, event, and internal applications into four groups with smaller instances and separate Varnish, EC2 web, and RDS layers.

Database incidents subsequently remained confined to the affected group, simplifying troubleshooting and improving site stability.
